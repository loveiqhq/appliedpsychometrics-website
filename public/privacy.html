We use this data only to deliver the website and keep it secure, never to identify you or build profiles. Vercel may process data in the United States. Vercel is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and transfers are also covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

If you email us, we use your address and message to reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns a contract with us, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise. Links to loveiq.org lead to our consumer brand LoveIQ, which has its own privacy policy. Those links include a note in the address saying they came from this website; it identifies this website, not you.

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16), erased (Art. 17) or restricted (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). To use any of these rights, email hello@loveiq.org.

You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (datenschutz-berlin.de).
