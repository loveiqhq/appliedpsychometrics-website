2. Cookies and your choice

On your first visit a banner asks whether we may use analytics cookies. Until you accept, the site stores only the cookie that remembers your choice and loads no analytics tools. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time, with effect for the future: use the round button in the bottom left corner of every page, or . Withdrawing reloads the page without analytics.

We use this data only to deliver the website and keep it secure, never to identify you or build profiles.
